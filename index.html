Software Defined Radio

ASR34 is a SDR developer kit, designed for advanced RF experimentation, rapid prototyping and customer-specific integration. It is intended for engineers who need a modular hardware base rather than a commodity board.

FeaturesSpecificationsDaughterboards

Platform features

ASR34 is built as a development platform for advanced RF work, lab validation and integration tasks. The design supports modular expansion and can be adapted for customer requirements where needed.

  • Four sample-aligned receive and four transmit converter channels
  • Single 64 MHz master clock distributed by an AD9513 to both converters, the FPGA and the daughterboards
  • Modular RF front ends on four plug-in daughterboard slots
  • Two transformer-coupled direct ADC inputs on SMA, usable without daughterboards
  • Two external GPIO headers for antenna switches, relays or other 5 V control logic
  • Customer-specific integration support available

Daughterboards

RF coverage comes from plug-in daughterboards, or from a simple direct interface to the ADCs for injecting raw signals: transformer-coupled, 1 to 250 MHz. For DC to 30 MHz, an external AD8137 or similar differential driver is recommended. Compatibility with Ettus USRP1-series daughterboards is to be confirmed.

aex is the companion RF daughterboard in development: with two boards fitted, ASR34 becomes a four-channel coherent receiver from 1 MHz to 6 GHz. The aex TX path is used for calibration and test; receive-only variants are available.
