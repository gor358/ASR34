ASR34 Software Defined Radio Developer Kit

ASR34 is a limited-run SDR developer kit designed in Cadence OrCAD PCB Designer for advanced RF experimentation, rapid prototyping and customer-specific integration. It is intended for engineers who need a modular hardware base rather than a commodity board.

01Platform features

ASR34 is built as a development platform for advanced RF work, lab validation and integration tasks. The design supports modular expansion and can be adapted for customer requirements where needed.

  • Limited-run SDR developer kit, USRP1-class architecture
  • Designed in Cadence OrCAD PCB Designer
  • Four sample-aligned receive and four transmit converter channels
  • Modular RF front ends on four plug-in daughterboard slots
  • Customer-specific integration support available
  • Suitable for advanced prototyping and low-volume builds

03Daughterboards

RF coverage comes from plug-in daughterboards. aebx is the companion RX/TX pair in development: with two pairs fitted, ASR34 becomes a four-channel coherent receiver from 1 MHz to 6 GHz with two transmit channels.

04Design data

For full-batch buyers purchasing all 35 units, design data can be supplied in ASCII/interchange format for import into compatible PCB tools.

The design data is available to full-batch buyers who need to continue the work in supported EDA environments.

05Why this release is priced higher

This is a low-volume engineering batch. The unit price reflects design finalisation, handling, testing, assembly coordination and technical support across a limited number of boards.

Buyers are not only purchasing a board; they are getting a platform with modular scope, engineering context and the option for future adaptation.
